# DSC 106 Final Rubric Checklist

Before final submission, verify:

## Website
- GitHub Pages URL works.
- Repo is public.
- Website loads without console errors.
- D3 visualizations render correctly.
- Video is linked or embedded.

## Hook
- The project opens with an interesting research question or attention-grabbing statement.

## Storytelling
- The page follows an and-but-therefore structure.
- The story includes surprise, personalization, or emotion.
- The story is not just a dashboard.

## Visual Encodings
- Encodings are appropriate for the data.
- No misleading axes.
- No overplotting unless handled with interaction.
- Labels and legends are clear.

## Interaction
- Interactions are polished and bug-free.
- Interactions help users discover patterns.
- Interactions are not decorative only.

## Annotations
- Important findings are directly annotated.
- Shading, labels, or callouts guide interpretation.

## Takeaway
- The ending clearly states what viewers should learn.
- The takeaway connects back to the visual evidence.

## Viewing Experience
- Text is readable.
- Charts fit on a laptop screen.
- No horizontal scrolling.
- Tooltips are usable.
- Colors have strong contrast.

## Demo Video
- YouTube link works.
- Video is 2 minutes or less.
- Video starts with a hook.
- Video explains what the visualization allows viewers to understand.
- Video ends with a takeaway.
